Good to know

A few common questions.

Does it replace my kitchen tap?+
Yes. Your HydrateServe tap replaces your existing mixer - you keep normal hot and cold, and add filtered, chilled, sparkling and boiling water on demand from the one tap. Prefer to keep your existing tap? We can also add the HydrateServe tap as a separate, additional tap.
What does a system cost?+
It depends on the functions you choose. You can build your system online for an instant, all-inclusive price, or ask us for a no-obligation quote.
Where do you install?+
Right across New Zealand. Our own technicians design, install and maintain every system.
Is the boiling water safe around children?+
Yes - the boiling side has a child-safe control, so it can't be triggered by accident.
How is the system looked after?+
It needs very little maintenance - CO2 can be ordered online, and replacement filters from our store.
What warranty does it come with?+
Watermark-certified taps carry a 5-year warranty, and the under-bench system comes with a 2-year warranty. Extended warranties are available.
